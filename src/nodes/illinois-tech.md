Illinois Tech started with a sermon. In 1890 Frank Gunsaulus preached the "Million Dollar Sermon," and meat packer Philip Armour gave a million dollars to found the Armour Institute. It merged with the Lewis Institute in 1940 to become the Illinois Institute of Technology. Mies van der Rohe ran the architecture school from 1938 to 1958 and designed the campus, including S. R. Crown Hall, now a National Historic Landmark.[^claude]

**1981 – 1982**

- Engineering / pre-med program. Honours Chemistry, independent study Calculus.
- Honours’ coursework in Electrical and Electronics Engineering.
