**June 2017 – November 2018** · Full time, Philadelphia, PA

- Managed external and internal resources focused toward improving the user experience of the ROAR Personal Safety Service. The Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) connectivity used by ROAR’s custom safety button was made faster, more reliable, and more robust. The Android app was dramatically improved in reliability and design using Android best practices.
- Recruited and led a great software team to transfer the ROAR iOS and Android app development from outside contractors to internal mobile developers, resulting in better quality control and faster release cycles. Released iOS and Android apps with a dramatically improved Athena safety button experience. Wrote an Athena BLE specification document to coordinate firmware and mobile software. Mentored junior iOS and Android developers.
- Successfully conceived of and proposed the “ROAR Safety Service for Hospitality” to the senior management team. This became the basis for the company’s pivot to B2B. It was renamed ROAR *AlwaysOn* and is now a patented solution helping staff feel safer in thousands of hotel rooms. The proposal allowed ROAR to earn the very important SAAS revenue as well as revenue for COGS and installs.

## The pivot

Athena was a good product in a hard market. We were having trouble selling it. Poor market fit. So we were looking for a solution.

Then I visited Howard University in Washington, DC, and realized how hard it is for university security to find someone in trouble when that person is indoors. They don't necessarily know where they are. There may be no Wi-Fi. No cellular. Picture a library basement, surrounded by metal shelves. That's a terrible place to need help.

We already had a BLE button. What if we paired it with a new kind of **BLE mesh**, small low-energy nodes spread through the building, each at a known location? Press the button and the mesh knows which room you're in. It would solve these customer problems in a way that hadn't been done before, using what we were already good at: BLE buttons, app development, tracking, and backend.

I pitched it to the founding team. In November 2018 it became [ROAR](/n/roar/), and I became one of its [co-founders](/n/roar-cofounder/).[^claude]
