Westtown is a Quaker school on 600 acres in Westtown, PA. Philadelphia Quakers opened it in 1799 as a boarding school for boys and girls together, 20 of each in the first group. It is believed to be the oldest continuously operating boarding school in the country, and today it is a K-12 day and boarding school.[^claude]

I was a student there from 1978 to 1981.[^claude] See also: [Westtown Game Console](/n/westtown-game-console/).
