**2001 – Present** · West Chester, PA

Founding MOEDAE LLC in 2001. MOEDAE stands for Mechanical, Optical, Electrical, Design Automation. A consulting service for technical development projects, currently primarily software for Apple devices requiring hardware integration such as bluetooth low energy devices at one end and cloud integration at the other.

- A sole business owner who slowly built up a staff and trusted service providers to grow the business.
- MOEDAE provided consulting services specializing in the development or refinement of products which required the fusion of mechanical, electrical, and software/firmware: a holistic multi-disciplinary approach to product development.

## R&D design engineering

- Used SolidWorks and MathCad to design sunglasses with an embedded HD video recording system for the consumer market. Included an innovative design for electro-mechanical image stabilization.
- Consulted on process for servicing and remanufacturing 20W, 19 element, 2400dpi infrared laser diode imaging head for a printing plate imaging product.
- Consulted on design of replacement service modules for an automated printing plate media loading product.
- Worked with clients to develop custom automation to improve their business process and reduce costs.
- Machined prototype parts in my own shop.

## Bluetooth Low Energy and Apple platforms

- Solution provider to [Qualcomm Life](/n/qualcomm-life-consultant/) and other companies, for the development of easy to use, best practice mobile apps for innovative Bluetooth Low Energy solutions.
- Co-inventor on Bluetooth Low Energy related patents.
- Extensive experience in Bluetooth Low Energy firmware development, mobile platform APIs, and user experience.
- Architect, designer, and developer of multiple macOS apps using Objective-C and Swift.
- Custom iOS and Android apps for demonstrating the potential use cases of the client’s custom BLE hardware. UI and BLE device communication for a BLE based pregnancy digital assay. Wrote a BlueGiga script running on a BLE hardware SDK in order to implement and test the iOS app without the actual client BLE hardware.
- User interfaces and BLE management for numerous BLE based devices, including dynamic charts.
- iOS and Android implementations using Java, RX and ObjC.
- WebKit, KeyChain and Swift to implement an iOS app wrapper for a RESTful Clojure web app. The goal was to store the user credentials on the iOS side to allow the user to auto-log in to the web app.
- Independently developing hardware & firmware for a standard NEMA 5-15P to IEC-320-C13 power cable able to be controlled by BLE/Wi-Fi.

Designed a UIStackView style framework and UICollectionView style drag & drop a year prior to the release of Apple’s versions. Unfortunately, the UI and L-System concepts are still too complex for most users. ([FractalScapes technical details](/n/the-tech-below-the-surface/))
