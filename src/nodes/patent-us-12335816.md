The family keeps growing. This is the second continuation of [US 11,128,987](/n/patent-us-11128987/), by way of [US 11,812,339](/n/patent-us-11812339/). It all goes back to the same 2019 provisional.

Claim 1 walks the whole chain. A mobile device detects a condition and sends it over Bluetooth to a stationary device at a known location. The stationary device relays it across a low-energy Bluetooth mesh to a gateway. The gateway hands it to a server, which uses that known location to pinpoint where the condition happened and raise an alert.[^claude]
