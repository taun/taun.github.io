**2013 – 2017** · Contract through MOEDAE LLC

- Provide services to Qualcomm Life Inc. (QCL) to develop iOS and Android applications capable of showcasing QCL’s innovative products such as bluetooth low energy health sensors and cloud infrastructure. Work with QCL to develop the best allocation of functionality between the sensor firmware and the user application in order to provide the best overall user interaction.
- Consulted with Qualcomm Life (QCL) and Qualcomm Technologies Inc (QTI) in the development of numerous Bluetooth low energy hardware/software devices. Role was as a BLE expert with particular experience in the operating system implementations of both Android and iOS as well as user interaction design.
- In order to speed up and improve the development process, MOEDAE used standard BLE development kits to simulate the BLE api’s of potential new QCL hardware in order to be able to test and write the mobile app without actual QCL hardware.
- Co-invented a USPTO Bluetooth Low Energy related patent, improving pairing and battery life for medical devices. ([US 10,880,932](/n/patent-us-10880932/))
