**1993 – 1996** · Vancouver, BC

- Lead a multi-disciplinary research and development team of 40+ engineers and 40+ manufacturing and support people in the development of Creo's largest platesetter, the VLF (Very Large Format).
- Worked with marketing and the project manager to set product requirements. Performed budget, scheduling and ROI forecasts, led design meetings.
- Responsibilities included ensuring that the product met government electrical and safety standards for Europe, Canada, and the USA. [CSA, UL, FCC, ISO 9001]
- The design specifications were for a Platesetter capable of loading, imaging and unloading to a plate processor, a 58 inch by 80 inch 20 thou thick aluminum printing plate, in 12 minutes. The image dot positions were to be repeatable to 0.35 thou (8 microns) from plate to plate and 1.2 thou (30 microns) from machine to machine.
- Concurrently, worked as a Senior Design Engineer on system pneumatics, castings, extrusions, electrical, electronics, transport systems, system integration, manufacturing and much more.
- Consistently ranked in the top 10% of Creo engineering staff by peers and supervisors.
