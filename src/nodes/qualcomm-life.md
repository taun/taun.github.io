Qualcomm Life was Qualcomm's bet on connected health. Qualcomm formed it in December 2011 in San Diego, out of its Wireless Health business, with a $100M investment fund on the side. The heart of it was **2net**, a cloud platform plus a hub for collecting, storing, and displaying data from medical devices.

In 2015 Qualcomm Life bought Capsule Technologie, a hospital device connectivity company serving more than 1,930 hospitals in 38 countries. In 2019 Francisco Partners bought Qualcomm Life and renamed it **Capsule Technologies**. In 2021 Philips bought Capsule for US$635M, and it is now part of Philips.[^claude]

From 2013 to 2017, MOEDAE built iOS and Android apps for Qualcomm Life's Bluetooth Low Energy health sensors. One of those projects became [US 10,880,932](/n/patent-us-10880932/).[^claude]
