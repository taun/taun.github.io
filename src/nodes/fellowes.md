If you have ever packed up an office, you have probably held a Fellowes product. The **Bankers Box**.

Fellowes started in Chicago in 1917. Harry Fellowes, a tailor, paid $121 for a record storage box idea from Walter Nickel, who had just been drafted into World War I. When Walter came home, Harry made him a 50/50 partner. That box became The Bankers Box Company. The company became Fellowes in 1983, as it moved into business machines and computer accessories, and Fellowes Brands in 2015.

Today they make shredders, laminators, binding machines, workspace and ergonomic products, office furniture, and commercial air purifiers. It is still privately held and run by the Fellowes family, now in the fourth generation, from headquarters in Itasca, Illinois.[^claude]

## Fellowes Array

Array is where the software comes in. It is a networked indoor air quality system, launched in 2023.

- **Clean air.** H13 True HEPA filtration.
- **Sensing.** PM2.5, PM10, CO2, TVOCs, temperature, humidity, pressure, and occupancy.
- **Connected.** Each unit talks to the cloud over LTE.
- **Visible.** Facility managers monitor, schedule, and maintain the fleet from the Viewpoint dashboard. In 2025 Array added building management system (BMS) integration over BACnet/IP.

Hardware, firmware, cellular, cloud, and a dashboard. A whole IoT SaaS stack inside a company that had never had a software engineer on staff. In January 2024, I became the first one. ([Staff Software Engineer, Fellowes](/n/fellowes-array/))[^claude]
