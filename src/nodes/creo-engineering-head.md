**1995 – 1999** · Vancouver, BC

- Hired and mentored 40+ excellent successful engineers and support personnel.
- Initiated, planned and implemented the replacement of AutoCAD with SolidWorks as the official Creo MCAD software. When I left, Creo had over 60 SolidWorks licenses and was the largest user of SolidWorks on the West Coast of Canada.
- Initiated and led the evaluation of Product Data Management (PDM) software.
- Helped implement an in-house GD&T (Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing) training program.
- Duties: Responsible for the development of mechanical engineering at Creo. This included recruitment, training, engineering standards (ANSI and ISO), peer review, salary reviews, performance reviews and mentoring. The role is awarded based on peer respect for technical proficiency, diplomacy and the ability to 'get things done.' In all but the last year, the section head duties were performed concurrent with Senior Design Engineer duties.
