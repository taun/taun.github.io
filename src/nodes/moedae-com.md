MOEDAE LLC was incorporated in 2001 in West Chester, PA, and it is still going. MOEDAE stands for Mechanical, Optical, Electrical, Design Automation.

A consulting service for technical development projects: products which required the fusion of mechanical, electrical, and software/firmware. A holistic multi-disciplinary approach to product development. In recent years, primarily software for Apple devices requiring hardware integration such as bluetooth low energy devices at one end and cloud integration at the other.

Before MOEDAE, I worked as an independent consultant, for example on the [NMR pulse programmer](/n/nmr-pulse-programmer/).[^claude]
