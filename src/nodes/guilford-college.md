Guilford College is a Quaker founded college in Greensboro, North Carolina. It opened in 1837 as New Garden Boarding School, coeducational from the start, and became Guilford College in 1888. The woods on campus were a meeting point on the Underground Railroad. It is the only Quaker founded college in the southeastern US.[^claude]
