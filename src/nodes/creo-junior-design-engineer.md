**1992 – 1993** · Vancouver, BC

<!-- Taun: these bullets come from the 2003 resume, where they cover all of the Creo design work from 1991 to 1999. Move or trim them to fit this role. -->

- Designed precision lens mounts, auto-focus mechanisms, imaging engines, production jigs and fixtures, test jigs, shock and vibration attenuation mounts, small precision parts to 7 foot aluminum castings. Principal design areas included CNC lathe, mill, stamped, pneumatics, castings, extrusions, electrical, electronics, transport systems, system integration, manufacturing and much more. Designs were created in 3D using AutoCAD and SolidWorks. Wrote AutoCAD scripts, used MS Excel to create SolidWorks design tables for assemblies. Created detailed drawings with GD&T standards. Machined prototype and test pieces.
- Mathematically modeled: thermal and vibration effects on system stability and accuracy, the effect of load stresses on system accuracy using MathCAD, SolidWorks and Excel.
- Conceived of and developed concept to replace a large precision aluminum casting with extrusions to reduce costs by 60%.
