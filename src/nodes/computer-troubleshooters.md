Computer Troubleshooters is an IT services franchise for small businesses. Wilson and Suzanne McOrist started it in Australia in the late 1990s, and Chip Reaves brought it to the US in 1999. By 2006 it had more than 450 locations in 22 countries. In 2016 it became part of CPR Cell Phone Repair, and the brand is still around today.

In 2003 I opened the West Chester, PA franchise.[^claude]
