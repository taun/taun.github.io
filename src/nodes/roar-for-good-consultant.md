**May – June 2017** · Hourly consultant, on a trial basis

- When hired by ROAR for Good, they were in dire straits. After 3 years of development time, they had a hard deadline to produce 2500 Bluetooth low energy (BLE) Nordic nrf51 based hardware devices in 2 weeks and the firmware for the devices didn’t work. Within the 2 weeks, the problems were investigated, a solution was implemented and production was able to proceed without penalty. The next few months were then spent dramatically improving the firmware, and mobile app so that they each did what they were best at, creating a much better user experience. For example, the BLE device knows its state and can communicate that state to the mobile app making development of the mobile app much easier and their interactions more robust. This had not been done making for an overly complicated system which would still get “out of sync.”
