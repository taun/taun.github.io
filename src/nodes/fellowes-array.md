**January 2024 – Present** · Itasca, IL

- First software engineer hired in the company's 114-year history. Responsible for guiding and reviewing the work of a 14-member team of external software consultants in the continuous development of Fellowes’ 3-year-old IoT-enabled SaaS model for the Indoor Air Quality Management product, Fellowes Array, leveraging distributed systems principles.
- Guided the Project & Product Managers in implementing best practices to improve software development output quality and efficiency by improving the CI, deployment pipelines, and workflow. Guided the implementation and documentation of the desired software test and release cycles. Eliminated the constant consultant rework due to unclear software requirements by working with the business team and software team to translate business requirements into software implementation needs based on software architecture best practices and systems engineering principles.
- Proposed and guided a change in the customer-facing web dashboard architecture, which improved the product user experience from needing unnecessary intervention every two weeks to zero user interventions, enhancing reliability.
- Re-architected the IoT platform to increase the Air Quality sensor hardware system's cellular LTE connectivity by five times. Increased over-the-air (OTA) update success and general system restart success from ~80% to 100% over 6 months. Anything less than 100% is considered a failure and would require a customer service intervention, demonstrating a commitment to reliability.
- Implemented processes to support developer technical development and aid in mentoring.
