**ROAR** is the B2B company that grew out of [ROAR for Good](/n/roarforgood/) in November 2018. Instead of selling a safety button to consumers, ROAR sells worker safety as a service, with indoor alert location tracking. Press a button and help knows exactly where you are. Even deep inside a building where GPS and cell signals give up.

The co-founders are [Yasmine Mustafa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yasmine_Mustafa), Rich Nelson, Mahmoud Odeh, and me. How the idea came about is on [Head of Software, ROAR for Good](/n/roar-for-good/). The legal name is still ROAR for Good. The brand is just ROAR.[^claude]

## AlwaysOn

Hotels came first. Housekeepers often work alone in guest rooms, and hotels were being asked to give them panic buttons. **AlwaysOn** put a BLE button on each worker and a mesh of small, low-cost Bluetooth nodes through the building, so an alert arrives with the room and floor. By March 2021, ROAR devices were in about 3,000 hotel rooms, with a deal signed for more than 11,000. The Bluetooth mesh became ROAR's [first patent](/n/patent-us-11128987/) in September 2021.[^claude]

## Today

The same service moved from hospitality into healthcare and behavioral health with very few changes. ROAR lists more than 300 customer organizations. It is a Certified B Corp and a certified women-owned business, and still operating.[^claude]
