**March 2020 – October 2022** · Philadelphia, PA

- Oversaw ROAR's product roadmap, technical direction, and recruitment of the product development team while also making hands-on coding contributions, demonstrating technical leadership.
- Earned a USPTO patent for the core technology, now safeguarding over 1 million individuals by inventing and leading a technical team of 6 to bring to market ROAR's flagship safety product while collaborating with senior management, sales, customer support, operations, and customers in the budget, timeline, and roadmap, showcasing systems engineering, leadership, and collaboration skills.
- Successfully proposed and led a cross-functional team of 8 in the development of a new, custom IoT edge gateway to replace expensive third-party cellular access points and unreliable, hard-to-manage gateways, which enhanced ROAR's SaaS safety service's reliability, margins, and competitive advantage.
- Reduced hardware costs by over 50%, reduced lead time by 50%, improved hardware quality control, installation quality control, and both internal and external customer satisfaction by leading the team to create an innovative new total lifecycle process from tooling to manufacturing to installation to end-of-life, code-named “Crush ‘em.” The cross-functional team for this process comprised UI/UX designers, hardware engineers, product managers, operations, customer service, and customers. Both manufacturers and installers referred to the new process as “genius,” highlighting strong collaboration skills and systems engineering.
- Established a CI/CD/production/stage/development infrastructure with reliability of better than 99.9% uptime from day one of rollout, which was only 6 months after the new service development go-ahead, demonstrating expertise in DevOps, software architecture, distributed systems, and cross-functional leadership.
