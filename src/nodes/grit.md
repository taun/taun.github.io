[Grit](https://www.angeladuckworth.com/grit) by Angela Duckworth.

## About

Angela is the Rosa Lee and Egbert Chang Professor of Psychology at the University of Pennsylvania. My current home town. Go Birds! _Grit_ is the result of 10 years of research into the psychological perspective on what separates high achievers from the rest of us. Or as the subtitle states, **THE POWER of PASSION and PERSEVERANCE**

It took her 10 years of research, fortunately, it only took me a couple of months to get through it. It was lots of fits and starts but I know it was making an impact because I couldn’t stop describing the study results to people. For the whole month. Everyone. All the time. Even strangers. Maybe it resonated with me because it validated how I already live my life. It gave me permission to not change what I’m doing. At least, not at the macro level. Big picture stuff.

## I learned

In chapter 7, Practice, she explains how it takes regular long term practice to excel, and when that practice is performed consistently, it becomes easy. It becomes automatic. 

## I changed

I explained to my manager how Angela’s research uncovered the importance of consistent practice in order to excel. Consistent means, the same time, duration, and place, at a regular interval. Practice is what you do to get better at your craft. I described how actively developing a software product is our “practice”. How in the interest of being better at our craft, we too needed consistent practice. And proposed the team be given 3 hours of uninterrupted, meeting free, time, Monday through Friday from 9:00am to 12:00pm. He agreed to give it a try. We had to ask others to move a number of meetings but once I described the goal, they too were happy to move the meetings.

## Result

It’s only been 2 weeks but **wow**. We are getting so much more done and being so much happier doing it. We still have the same number of meetings but they are all in the afternoon. Which gives us the consistency that makes practicing easier. In our case, knowing that when you sit down at 9:00am, you will be interruption free for the next 3 hours really makes a difference in motivation and focus. It’s much more pleasant to start working on something difficult if you know you won’t be interrupted just as you are getting close to a solution.

As a software team lead, I have tried and failed to solve this problem for the last 10 years. _Grit_ finally gave me both a method, and evidence to back it up when discussing the goal with management.
