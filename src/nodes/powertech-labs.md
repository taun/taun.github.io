Powertech Labs is the testing and research arm of BC Hydro. It grew out of BC Hydro's research division around 1979 and sits in Surrey, BC, with 19 laboratories on 11 acres. Utilities, electrical equipment makers, and automotive companies send it work: high voltage, high power, mechanical and materials testing, applied chemistry, and power system analysis. Its materials lab tests protective coatings for corrosion and weathering. It also claims the world's first 700 bar hydrogen fast-fill fueling station.

I spent the summer of 1990 there as a co-op student, inspecting and testing protective coatings.[^claude]
