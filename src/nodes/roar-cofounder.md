**November 2018 – March 2020** · Philadelphia, PA

- Conceived, and led the cross-functional development of ROAR's flagship safety product, now the market's best, safeguarding over 1 million individuals and earning a USPTO patent. The proposed new ROAR Safety Service was based on analyzing market research conducted by the team, as well as meeting personally with potential customers to gain empathy for their needs.
- Worked collaboratively with our hardware & firmware engineer to successfully architect and implement a prototype ROAR AlwaysOn Service in less than 6 months while learning to use the backend implementation language, Elixir, for the first time. Was hands-on as a developer to implement real-time handling of IoT MQTT messages in Elixir using AWS IoT Core, Phoenix UI & API, Absinthe, AWS RDS, PostgreSQL, GraphQL, and AWS DevOps for frontend and backend while also managing contractors for the necessary iOS, Android, and Phoenix development.
- Recruited and led a multidisciplinary team from the initial concept stage to a successful pilot deployment of ROAR AlwaysOn service in under 6 months. Meeting this milestone allowed the company to raise significant additional investment for continued company growth. Personally assisted with the pilot install on-site in order to meet with customers and evaluate the initial customer and installer experience.
