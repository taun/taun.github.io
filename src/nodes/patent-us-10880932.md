More and more home medical devices report their data to a phone over Bluetooth Low Energy. The phone is the **central**, the medical device is the **peripheral**. It works great. Until someone else needs the data.

The patient comes in for a visit, their phone in their pocket, still connected to the medical device. The nurse's tablet can't get in. As the patent puts it, "this central/peripheral relationship can prevent other devices from connecting with the medical device."

The fix is a polite "excuse me." The clinician's device broadcasts a **central priority** advertisement packet. The patient's phone hears it and stops trying to connect to the medical device for a period of time. That opens the door for the clinician's device to connect and pull the data.[^claude]
