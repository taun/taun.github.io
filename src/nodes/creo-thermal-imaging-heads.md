**1997 – 1998** · Vancouver, BC

- Technical lead for a 20+ person research and development team in the continued development of the company's core technology, the Thermal Imaging Head, used in all Creo’s platesetter products.
- As project engineer, successfully lead a multi-disciplinary engineering team in the design of a 2nd generation 40W 19 element infrared laser diode thermal sensitive media imaging product for the printing industry. Design included enclosure, kinematic mounts, static and dynamic optical elements, passive and forced thermal management, motion control and custom manufacturing processes.
- Analyzed and solved thermal management problems with a 1st generation 20W, 19 laser diode infrared 2400dpi imaging product.
- Improved Thermal Imaging head quality through the application of precision opto-mechanical design principals, tolerance analysis and improved procedures. This lead to a reduced component rejection rate and a statistically higher performance of passed components.
- Invented an innovative production lens-assembly process capable of <0.05 thou (sub-micron) resolution and 0.1 thou (2 micron) repeatability. Which reduced per piece process time from person hours to minutes.
