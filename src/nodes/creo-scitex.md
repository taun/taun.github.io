CreoScitex is what [Creo](/n/creo/) became in March 2000, when it bought Scitex Corp.'s digital prepress business for 13.25 million Creo shares, about US$537M. Scitex ended up owning about 27% of Creo.

The combined company had about 4,200 employees, with R&D and manufacturing in Vancouver, Herzliya, Israel, and Bedford, Massachusetts. CreoScitex was a division name. In 2002 the company became Creo Inc., and in 2005 Kodak bought it.[^claude]
