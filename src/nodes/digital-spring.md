Digital Spring was a small startup with a simple goal. Stop killing your plants. Jim Canyon founded it around 2012, and I joined as a founding junior partner, 1 of 3.[^claude]

The product was the **Daisy Sensor**. Push it into the soil next to your plant and it tracks soil moisture, light, and temperature, then talks to your phone over Bluetooth Low Energy. The moisture sensing was non-contact, using eField technology. A December 2014 Kickstarter, "Give your indoor plants a voice to stay healthy," was fully funded by February 2015, with early backers getting 2 sensors for $34. Bigger versions were planned too: Daisy Pro for large plants and trees, and ECCO for farms, lawns, and golf courses.[^claude]

After 4 years of development, production started in April 2017 with 2000 units made in China. The [Daisy Sensor iOS app](https://apps.apple.com/app/id916216905) was last updated in August 2017, and the company appears to be dormant.[^claude]
